# ShopSphere Return, Exchange & Warranty Policy

Effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all orders shipped within India. All day counts are calendar days measured from the date and time the carrier recorded the order as **delivered**. The date the order was placed does not matter.

## POL-1 Apparel & Footwear: 30-day return window

Apparel (jackets, base layers, hydration packs, clothing accessories) and Footwear (trail shoes, running shoes) can be returned for a **full refund to the original payment method within 30 days of delivery**.

Conditions:
- The item must be **unworn and unwashed**, with all **original tags attached**.
- Footwear must be returned in the original shoebox. Shoes with outdoor wear on the sole (mud, scuffs, worn tread) are considered worn and are not accepted.
- A return request creates an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number. Ship the item using the prepaid label issued for that RMA within 5 days.

Examples: trail shoes delivered 4 days ago and still unworn are eligible for a full refund. A jacket delivered 36 days ago is outside this window (see POL-4).

## POL-2 Electronics: strict 14-day return window

Electronics (earbuds, headphones, power banks, chargers) have a **strict 14-day return window** from delivery. There is no grace period for electronics after day 14.

- **Unopened electronics** (factory seal intact) returned within 14 days receive a **full refund** to the original payment method.
- **Opened non-audio electronics** (for example power banks) can be returned within 14 days if complete with all accessories and undamaged.
- **Opened audio products that go in or on the ear** are covered by the hygiene and seal rule in POL-3.

After 14 days, electronics are only covered by the manufacturer warranty (POL-6).

## POL-3 In-ear audio hygiene & seal rule (earbuds)

For hygiene reasons, **in-ear audio products (wireless earbuds, in-ear monitors, earphones) can only be returned once the box is opened if the product is defective.**

- **Opened and working (change of mind, fit, sound preference): not returnable.** The request is rejected as non-returnable.
- **Opened and defective** (will not charge, will not pair, one bud silent, crackling, battery fails) within 14 days of delivery: returnable for a full refund or a replacement. Describe the defect when raising the return.
- **Unopened** (hygiene seal intact) within 14 days: full refund, as in POL-2.

Example: AeroBuds Pro earbuds delivered 6 days ago with the box opened can be returned only if they are defective.

## POL-4 31-45 day grace policy: exchange / store-credit voucher

Apparel and Footwear items requested **between day 31 and day 45 after delivery** are **not eligible for a refund to the original payment method**. Instead they qualify for an **Instant Exchange / Store Credit Voucher**:

- **Standard members:** voucher worth **80%** of the price paid.
- **VIP members:** voucher worth **90%** of the price paid.

Rules:
- Item condition rules from POL-1 still apply (unworn, tags attached).
- The voucher is issued instantly, is valid for **180 days**, and can be used on any product in the store.
- Only one voucher is issued per order item. Asking again returns the same voucher code.
- The grace policy does **not** apply to Electronics or Hygiene items.
- After day 45, no return, refund or voucher is available.

Example: a Nimbus Down Jacket delivered 36 days ago cannot be refunded, but a VIP customer can receive a 90% store-credit voucher for it.

## POL-5 Personal care & hygiene: final sale once opened

Personal care and hygiene products (electric toothbrushes such as the PureSonic Electric Toothbrush, brush heads, grooming devices) are **final sale once the packaging is opened**. Opened hygiene items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive dead on arrival (DOA), in which case raise a warranty claim under POL-6.

Unopened hygiene items in their sealed packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.

## POL-6 Manufacturer warranty

- Electronics carry a **1-year manufacturer warranty** from the delivery date. Warranty claims cover manufacturing defects and are fixed by repair or replacement, not refund.
- Electric toothbrushes carry a **2-year warranty** on the handle (not brush heads).
- Footwear carries a **6-month warranty** against sole separation and seam failure under normal use.
- Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage beyond the stated rating, or normal wear.

## POL-7 Late delivery compensation

If an order is delivered, or is still in transit, **more than 24 hours after the promised delivery date** shown at checkout, the customer qualifies for a **₹500 courtesy coupon**.

- One coupon per order, valid for 90 days, minimum cart value ₹1,000.
- Applies to delays caused by the carrier, weather or hub congestion.
- Does not apply if the customer changed the delivery address or was unavailable for delivery.

## POL-8 Refund timelines & how returns work

1. Raise a return in chat or in My Orders. The system checks eligibility automatically against the delivery date and these rules.
2. If approved you receive an RMA number and a prepaid return label from our logistics partner.
3. Drop the parcel at a nearby drop-off point (smart locker, carrier hub or partner store) or schedule a pickup.
4. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the item passes inspection. UPI and wallet refunds are usually faster.

## POL-9 VIP membership benefits

VIP members receive a higher 90% grace-period voucher (instead of 80%), priority pickup for returns, and free express shipping on exchanges. All other return windows and condition rules are the same as for Standard members.
