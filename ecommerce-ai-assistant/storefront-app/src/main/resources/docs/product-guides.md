## SUMMIT-SHOE-01 SummitGrip Waterproof Trail Shoes

Category: Footwear. Price ₹7,499.

Built for wet, muddy and rocky trails. A waterproof breathable membrane keeps feet dry through puddles, stream crossings and monsoon downpours. The 5 mm multi-directional lugged rubber outsole sheds mud quickly and grips wet rock and slick clay. Gusseted tongue keeps out grit, and a rock plate protects against sharp stones.

Best for: **monsoon hiking, muddy trail running, Western Ghats treks, wet-weather day hikes.** Not ideal for: hot dry road running (the membrane runs warm).

Specs: 320 g per shoe, 8 mm drop, waterproof membrane, reflective heel tab, sizes UK 6-12.

## URBAN-RUN-01 UrbanStride Velocity Running Shoes

A lightweight road-running shoe with a breathable engineered mesh upper and a carbon-infused foam midsole for an energetic toe-off. **Not waterproof**: the mesh upper drains and dries fast but lets water in, so it is a poor choice for muddy or flooded trails.

Best for: **road running, tempo runs, treadmill, gym, dry city pavements, race day up to half marathon.** Not ideal for: mud, trails or monsoon puddles.

Specs: 240 g per shoe, 6 mm drop, flat road outsole, sizes UK 5-12.
