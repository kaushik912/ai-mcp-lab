# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping

Orders over $50 ship free within 5-7 business days. Expedited shipping
(2-3 business days) is available for a flat fee of $12.

## International Shipping

International orders take 10-15 business days and are subject to
customs fees charged by the destination country.

## Order Tracking

A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of the order shipping.
Use it on the carrier's website to see real-time delivery status.
